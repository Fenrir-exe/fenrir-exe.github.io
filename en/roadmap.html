Roadmap

There are no dates here — only the order. Details of each step appear in the devlog (in Russian).

Done

  • the game rules and match engine, an AI opponent;
  • the client: table, cards, effects, sound, tutorial, collection and decks;
  • art, sound and effects for the first card set;
  • accounts and a server for future online play.

Now

  • A closed test among friends: matches, a questionnaire, fixes based on the results.

Next

  • Online duels and an invite-only closed test;
  • balance of the cards through large AI-vs-AI runs;
  • a campaign through the story arcs — starting with the first one;
  • an online campaign;
  • ranking and card trading — that will be version 1.0.

Tidewill is an unofficial, non-commercial fan game. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Eiichiro Oda, Shueisha, Toei Animation or Bandai; One Piece belongs to its rights holders. The game is free. The art, sounds and music are made with the help of AI.
